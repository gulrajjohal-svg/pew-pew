Controls

Player 1 (keyboard): W / S move forward and back, A / D turn, F fire, Q next weapon, E heal (medkit or Extra HP potion).

Player 2 (same keyboard): Up / Down move, Left / Right turn, Enter fire, . (period) next weapon, / (slash) heal.

Phone or tablet: left joystick moves and turns, FIRE shoots, Weapon switches gun, Heal uses a medkit or Extra HP.

Anywhere: H shows or hides this list. Walk into golden chests to open them (they are inside buildings, on rooftops and towers too). Walk over items to pick them up. Walk up ramps to reach roofs and towers. Stay inside the purple storm circle. Auto-aim locks on to rivals near your crosshair.

Tap or press anywhere to close.
